💡 About TissueTag and printing tips
  • Labels follow the format ACRONYM 00001 within the range you choose.
  • Each QR code holds the same text printed on its label.
  • Column order fills one full column before the next, so you can cut strips and stack them; the sequence continues across pages.
  • Print at 100% scale with Background graphics turned on.
  • Your settings are remembered the next time you open the app.
